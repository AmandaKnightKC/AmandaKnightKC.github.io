About Me

I work with operational data — specifically how information moves between business systems and how to diagnose problems when records don’t match.

In employee benefits administration, I investigate eligibility, enrollment, and billing discrepancies across connected platforms and carrier files. This requires understanding structured datasets, tracing data flow between systems, and identifying root causes when outputs are incorrect.

This portfolio shows how I apply the same analytical thinking using Python and SQL to answer business questions such as performance measurement, KPI analysis, and identifying patterns in operational data, and identifying patterns in operational processes. The goal is to translate operational data into decisions, not just visualizations.

Specialties: Operational Data Analysis · Data Validation & Quality Checks · Cross-System Data Investigation · Root Cause Analysis · SQL · Python · Excel Analysis · Business Process Analysis · Healthcare & Insurance Data
